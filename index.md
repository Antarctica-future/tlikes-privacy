## Summary

Tlikes lets you manually sign in to TikTok Web and build a private library of Likes on your Android device. The developer does not operate an app backend, receive your library, or use advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Data handled on the device

The app stores the TikTok Web session managed by Android WebView; video identifiers, creator names, descriptions, publication times, durations, and relative Like order; import checkpoints and pseudonymous source hashes; preferences; and bounded technical diagnostics. These data remain in Android private app storage and are excluded from cloud backup and device transfer.

## Network communication and third parties

When you choose to open TikTok or play a video, the embedded WebView communicates directly with TikTok over HTTPS. TikTok may process sign-in details, cookies, device/network information, and page requests under its own privacy policy. The app does not read passwords, automate sign-in forms, extract cookies, or send stored library data to the developer.

## Sharing and export

The app does not automatically share or sell personal data. A library export is created only after you explicitly choose an output file with Android's document picker. A redacted diagnostic can likewise be exported, and a bounded player diagnostic can be copied to the clipboard, only after an explicit action. Exported files and clipboard contents are then controlled by you and the apps you choose.

## Retention and deletion

Data remain on the device until you delete them. On the app's welcome screen, choose **Delete local data** and confirm. Android then clears the complete private library, import checkpoints, preferences, diagnostics, cache, and TikTok Web session for this app. Uninstalling the app or using Android's **Clear storage** action has the same effect.

## Security

The app accepts only explicitly allowed HTTPS origins, cancels TLS errors, blocks cleartext traffic, validates messages crossing WebView boundaries, and keeps WebView debugging disabled in release builds. No security measure can eliminate all risk, and TikTok Web behavior can change independently of the app.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children. The final store listing and age targeting must be consistent with the developer's selected audience and TikTok's own eligibility requirements.
